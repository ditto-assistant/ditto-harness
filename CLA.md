## Why a CLA?

The CLA grants Omni Aura LLC the rights necessary to:

1. Offer ditto-harness under the AGPL-3.0-or-later for the open-source
   community.
2. Offer a Commercial License to organizations that cannot comply with the AGPL.
3. Relicense the project in the future if needed (e.g., to a more permissive
   license) without needing to contact every contributor.

Without a CLA, Omni Aura LLC could not legally offer the commercial license
option, because each contributor would retain independent copyright over their
contributions.
